## 🔴 CRITICAL: Do This FIRST (Takes 30 minutes)

### **Set Up Google Business Profile**

This is THE most important thing for local search!

**Why?** When someone searches "barbershop near me" in North Mankato, Google shows businesses from Google Business Profile FIRST.

**How to do it:**

1. **Go to:** https://business.google.com
2. **Click:** "Manage now"
3. **Enter:**
   - Business name: Family Ties Barbershop
   - Category: Barber shop
   - Address: 407 Range St, North Mankato, MN 56003
   - Phone: 507-720-0522
   - Website: https://family-ties-barbershop.vercel.app

4. **Verify:** Google will mail you a postcard (takes 5-7 days)
5. **Complete Profile:**
   - Upload 15+ photos (shop, barbers, haircuts)
   - Add all services and prices
   - Add business hours
   - Write description (mention North Mankato, Mankato, haircuts, fades, etc.)

**Result:** You'll appear on Google Maps when people search for barbershops!
