**Steps:**
1. Go to https://business.google.com
2. Click "Manage now" or "Add your business"
3. Enter business name: "Family Ties Barbershop"
4. Choose category: "Barber shop"
5. Add location: 407 Range St, North Mankato, MN 56003
6. Add phone: 507-720-0522
7. Add website: https://family-ties-barbershop.vercel.app
8. Verify your business (Google will mail a postcard with verification code)
9. Complete your profile:
   - Add business hours
   - Upload photos (at least 10-15 high-quality photos)
   - Add services and pricing
   - Write business description (use keywords naturally)
   - Add attributes (walk-ins welcome, wheelchair accessible, etc.)
