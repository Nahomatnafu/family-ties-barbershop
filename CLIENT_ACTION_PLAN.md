## 📋 Your Step-by-Step Action Plan

### 🔴 PRIORITY #1: Google Business Profile (Do This First!)

**Time Required:** 30-45 minutes  
**Cost:** FREE  
**Impact:** MASSIVE (This is 70% of local SEO success)

#### Why This Matters:

When someone searches "barbershop near me" on Google, the results show:
1. **Google Maps** (top of page) ← This is Google Business Profile
2. Regular search results (below the map)

**If you're not on Google Business Profile, you're invisible on Google Maps!**

#### How to Set It Up:

**Step 1: Create Your Profile**
1. Go to: https://business.google.com
2. Click "Manage now" or "Add your business"
3. Sign in with a Google account (use a business email if possible)

**Step 2: Enter Your Information**

Use this EXACT information (consistency is critical):

```
Business Name: Family Ties Barbershop
Category: Barber shop
Address: 407 Range St, North Mankato, MN 56003
Phone: (507) 720-0522
Website: https://family-ties-barbershop.vercel.app
```

**Step 3: Verify Your Business**
- Google will mail you a postcard with a verification code (takes 5-7 days)
- Enter the code when it arrives
- Your profile goes live!

**Step 4: Complete Your Profile (IMPORTANT!)**

After verification, add:

1. **Business Hours:**
   - Monday-Friday: 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM
   - Saturday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
   - Sunday: Closed

2. **Business Description** (use keywords naturally):
   ```
   Family Ties Barbershop is North Mankato's premier destination for precision 
   haircuts, modern fades, beard trims, and hot shaves. Located at 407 Range St, 
   we serve the Mankato area with expert barbers specializing in classic cuts, 
   modern styles, and kids haircuts. Walk-ins welcome! Book online or call 
   (507) 720-0522.
   ```

3. **Services** (add all of these):
   - Haircut - $37
   - Beard Trim - $20
   - Hot Towel Shave - $40
   - Kids Cut - $27
   - Haircut + Beard - $45
   - Lining - $15
   - Premium Grooming - $75

4. **Photos** (upload at least 15-20):
   - Shop exterior
   - Shop interior
   - Each barber (headshots)
   - Before/after haircuts
   - Customers getting haircuts
   - Your logo
   - Team photos

5. **Attributes** (select all that apply):
   - Walk-ins welcome
   - Wheelchair accessible
   - Free Wi-Fi
   - Accepts credit cards
   - Accepts debit cards
   - Street parking
   - etc.

**Step 5: Download the Google Business App**
- iOS: https://apps.apple.com/app/google-my-business/id853371336
- Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.gmb

This lets you manage your profile, respond to reviews, and post updates from your phone.

### 🔴 PRIORITY #2: Get Customer Reviews

**Time Required:** 2 minutes per customer
**Cost:** FREE
**Impact:** HUGE (Reviews = Rankings)

#### Why This Matters:

Google's algorithm heavily weighs customer reviews for local businesses:
- **0-10 reviews:** You're invisible
- **10-20 reviews:** You start appearing
- **20-50 reviews:** You're competitive
- **50+ reviews:** You dominate

**Each review is worth more than 10 backlinks for local SEO!**

#### How to Get Reviews:

**Method 1: Ask In-Person (Best Results)**

After every haircut, say:
```
"If you're happy with your cut, would you mind leaving us a Google review?
It really helps our small business!"
```

**Method 2: Text/Email Follow-Up**

Send this message after the appointment:
```
Hey [Name]! Thanks for coming to Family Ties today. If you loved your cut,
we'd really appreciate a Google review! Here's the link: [YOUR REVIEW LINK]

Thanks for supporting our local business!
- Family Ties Team
```

**How to Get Your Review Link:**

1. Go to your Google Business Profile
2. Click "Get more reviews"
3. Copy the short link (looks like: `https://g.page/r/XXXXX/review`)
4. Save this link and use it everywhere

**Pro Tips:**
- Ask your best customers first (they'll leave 5-star reviews)
- Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours (even negative ones)
- Thank customers for positive reviews
- Address concerns in negative reviews professionally
- Never offer incentives for reviews (against Google's policy)

**Goal:** 5 new reviews per month minimum
